# Roadmap and launch

Status: Draft — dates, scope, and commitments are open.

## Immediate research and prototype sequence

1. Test the save → find → connect outcome with a few people using their own permitted references; document failures and the terms they use to describe the task.
2. Prototype a hosted URL/image library with durable saves, processing states, search previews and manual board placement. Treat scope as a hypothesis, not a delivery commitment.
3. Evaluate retrieval quality, indexing latency and cost on a consented mixed-media sample before choosing models or infrastructure. Investigate extension capture and source permissions separately.
4. Test the quiet canvas and Surface Field against a static alternative, including reduced motion and keyboard access.

Detailed evidence and engineering questions: [reference research](09-reference-research.md).
