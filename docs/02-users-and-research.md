## Questions to investigate

Ask about recent behavior before asking people to predict whether they would use an idea.

1. Tell me about the last time you encountered this problem.
2. What did you do, step by step?
3. What was the hardest part, and what did it cost you?
4. What have you already tried? Why did you keep or abandon it?
5. How often does this happen, and how important is fixing it?
